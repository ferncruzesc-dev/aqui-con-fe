01 — About

Here, with faith.

aQui con fe is a place for thinking, making, remembering, questioning, creating, and being present.

“Ideas do not have to choose between intellect and spirit, history and art, research and story.”

aQui means “here.” Con fe means “with faith.”

Together, aQui con fe names a commitment to being present — with curiosity, with memory, with one another, and with the questions that shape our lives.

This space is rooted in scholarship and creative practice, while making room for faith in its many traditions and expressions.

It is also a space for the social arts: the stories, photographs, songs, poems, histories, archives, essays, performances, conversations, and everyday forms of knowledge through which communities understand themselves.
