Explore the Archive Enter the Journal ￼ the archive begins here image source ↗ IDEAS ◆ STORIES ◆ RESEARCH ◆ MEMORY ◆ MUSIC ◆ COMMUNITY 01 — ABOUT Here, with faith. aQui con fe is a place for thinking, making, remembering, questioning, creating, and being present. aQui means “here.” Con fe means “with faith.” Together, aQui con fe names a commitment to being present — with curiosity, with memory, with one another, and with the questions that shape our lives. This space is rooted in scholarship and creative practice, while making room for faith in its many traditions and expressions. It is also a space for the social arts: the stories, photographs, songs, poems, histories, archives, essays, performances, conversations, and everyday forms of knowledge through which communities understand themselves. “Ideas do not have to choose between intellect and spirit, history and art, research and story.” I Scholarship Research, essays, literature, history, education, law, culture, archives, and community knowledge. II Faith Reflections on belief, belonging, spiritual practice, justice, questions, and faith across traditions. III Arts Music, literature, poetry, photography, film, visual art, performance, and storytelling. IV Community Presence over status. Stories, resources, conversations, collaborations, and shared knowledge.
