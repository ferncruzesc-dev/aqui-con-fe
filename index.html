01 / About aQui con fe

A place to think, believe, create.

Grounded in presence rather than status, aQui con fe is a living space where scholarship, faith, art, memory, music, literature, history, and the social arts can meet.

aQui

aQui means here. con fe means with faith.

Together, the name describes a way of being: present with curiosity, present with questions, present with one another, and present with the stories we inherit and the stories we are still making.

This is a space where intellectual life does not have to separate itself from spiritual life, where history can live alongside imagination, and where art can be both beautiful and socially meaningful.

The archive is intentionally unfinished. There should always be another room to enter.

02 / The rooms

Come inside.

aQui con fe is imagined as a house of ideas: different rooms, different forms of attention, one shared place.

Scholarship, research, education, questions, reading, teaching, archives, and the ideas that keep unfolding. A place to take knowledge seriously without pretending that knowledge is ever finished.

Faith, reflection, devotion, doubt, ritual, spiritual practice, questions of belonging, and the many ways people search for meaning.

Literature, books, poetry, fiction, criticism, language, translation, intertextuality, and the strange architecture of the written word.

Music, playlists, artists, lyrics, sound, performance, music history, and the songs that become part of how we remember ourselves.

History, documents, testimony, oral histories, photographs, institutional records, migration, memory, and the things people choose to preserve.

Photography, visual art, film, performance, exhibitions, family archives, and images that hold more than words can sometimes carry.

Community, conversation, resources, collaboration, shared knowledge, and the belief that presence should not depend upon socioeconomic status.

Works in progress. Creative experiments. Research questions. Unfinished stories. Projects that are still becoming what they are.

The moments that stop us in our tracks: discoveries, beautiful accidents, strange connections, unexpected questions, and things worth saying — wait, look at this.

A visual and musical diary of ideas, places, books, people, questions, memories, and moments that refuse to stay still.

A room for presence, care, reflection, conversation, and returning to ourselves. A reminder that sometimes being here is enough.
