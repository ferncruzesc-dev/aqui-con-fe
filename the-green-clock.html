Poesia de Fe

The Green Clock

by F. Cruz Escalante

Strike! hand by hand, a tick-a-turn,
So quick and so keen, like a burn
That ignites when a match strikes firewood.
Some call the winter air magic,
Some believe it can never be understood;
The songs that breed are fierce classic,
With a hook or a chorus that reminds
That all the amenities that come,
Will only stand with what green binds;
Suddenly, the green hour sounds like a drum:
The Green Clock has the future all planned.
The Green Thumb--a part of a ticking hand.
